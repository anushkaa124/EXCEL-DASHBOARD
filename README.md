Crime analysis plays a pivotal role in urban planning, law enforcement, and public safety policymaking. This project, titled “Analysis of Crime Patterns in Los Angeles Using Excel Dashboards,” leverages a comprehensive dataset of crime incidents in Los Angeles to uncover patterns and trends that can inform stakeholders about crime distribution, victim demographics, and weapon usage. By utilizing Microsoft Excel’s robust analytical and visualization tools, the project aims to provide actionable insights through interactive dashboards.
The dataset includes detailed records of crimes, such as robbery, assault, battery, and vandalism, across various Los Angeles police divisions. The analysis focuses on temporal trends, geographic distribution, crime types, weapon involvement, and victim demographics to understand the dynamics of crime in the city.
Key Analytical Goals:
•	Analyze the distribution of crimes across months to identify seasonal trends.
•	Compare crime counts across different Los Angeles police divisions.
•	Examine the proportion of various crime types.
•	Investigate weapon usage and average victim age across crime areas.
•	Assess the gender distribution of victims across crime locations.
•	Develop interactive dashboards using Pivot Tables, Charts, Slicers, and Conditional Formatting to facilitate data exploration.
